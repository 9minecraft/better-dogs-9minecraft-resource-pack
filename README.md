# Better Dogs — Real World Dog Breeds in Vanilla Style

Better Dogs 9minecraft Resource Pack reskins standard Minecraft wolves into dozens of real-world dog breeds. This pack brings unique coats, markings, and body shapes while keeping the classic blocky look of the game. You can collect different pups like huskies, golden retrievers, and borzois during your adventures. Naming your tamed pet with a name tag also lets you pick your favorite breed directly.

Adds 41 different dog breeds with over 130 unique textures.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **MrBlueYeti**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Keeping a pack alive across releases is not a copy-paste job: each Minecraft version bumps the pack format and shuffles texture and model paths, so the tree gets re-mapped and every asset re-checked against the version it is built for. Anything the new release dropped is rebuilt rather than left pointing at a path that no longer exists.
